Direction

Direction is CC 82: 127 = clockwise, 0 = counter-clockwise. The motor brakes and restarts the other way. Turning the encoder past minimum speed also reverses it.

Scale

Root note lands on Track 1 / Red. The scale's intervals step forward, color by color, track by track, through Track 4 / Pink — shared by all 4 tracks. Edit the resulting notes per-track inside each track's Mapping section.

Enabled

Plays any MIDI controller or keyboard into Orbita, with no DAW in between. Everything goes out on channel 1, the only channel Orbita listens on. Map your controller's knobs to Orbita parameters below. Keep this page open while you play.

Forward notes

Hold a note and all 4 tracks play it instead of the color mapping: Track 1 at the played pitch, Tracks 2–4 one, two and three octaves higher. Release the note and tracks go back to colors.

Pass unmapped CCs

On: CCs that are not mapped below go to Orbita with the same number, so a controller already set to Orbita's CC numbers works directly. Off: only mapped CCs are sent.

Help · MIDI CC list

Orbita listens on USB MIDI, channel 1. Messages on other channels and on the 5-pin MIDI port are ignored. Orbita sends its own output (notes, CC, clock) on USB and on the 5-pin MIDI out.

Per track — send to Orbita

Device — send to Orbita

  • Note On / Off, channel 1 — while a note is held, every track plays it instead of its color mapping (Track 1 at pitch, Tracks 2–4 +1/+2/+3 octaves).
  • Settings changed by CC are lost on power off until you send CC 99 = 127 (or press Save to Orbita).

What Orbita sends

On the device

  • Encoder button — start / stop the motor.
  • Turn the encoder — speed. Keep turning below minimum speed (3 steps) to reverse direction.
  • Hold the encoder button while powering on — reset to factory settings.
  • Flash new firmware — hold BOOTSEL, plug in USB, drop the .uf2 file on the RPI-RP2 drive.
